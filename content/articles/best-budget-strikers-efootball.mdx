You do not need a featured pack legend to score in Division. Under 150k GP, the market still has finishers who punish poor defending — if you pick the right playstyle for your build-up.

## How we ranked them

We looked at three things only:

- **Finishing and weak foot** — can they bury a chance from either side?
- **Acceleration in the box** — first step to create separation.
- **Link-up options** — one-twos or hold-up, depending on your midfield.

Boosters and random form spikes were ignored. Consistency beats a one-week purple arrow.

## Five forwards worth the GP

### 1. The classic poacher

Low GP, high finishing, lives on the last shoulder. Pair with a creative AMF and through balls. Do not ask him to drop deep.

### 2. The hold-up nine

Stronger, slower, wins fouls and knockdowns. Ideal if your wingers make late runs. Use Target Man instructions and feed early crosses sparingly — cutbacks are better.

### 3. The dual-threat wide CF

Play him as a second striker in a 4-4-2 or false nine in a 4-2-3-1. He needs space to turn; smothering midfields shut him down.

### 4. The free-kick specialist

Slightly overpriced on finishing alone, but set pieces swing tight matches. If you create three free kicks a game, he pays for himself.

### 5. The rotation option

Buy him as a super-sub. Fresh legs against tired centre-backs in the 70th minute win more points than a starter who fades.

## Playstyles to match

| Your midfield style | Best CF playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Through balls | Goal Poacher |
| Crosses / cutbacks | Fox in the Box |
| Slow build | Target Man |
| Counter attacks | Deep-Lying Forward (as a foil) |

## Spending tip

Leave 20–30k GP in reserve for a manager change or a contract crisis. A 149k striker who empties the club is a squad-building own goal.
