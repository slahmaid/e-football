Alongside competitive modes, Konami partnered with **PlatinumGames** to refresh daily bonus content. The new **Daily Super Play** feature asks you to recreate spectacular football moments with simple timing inputs and a special player roster — described in [Konami’s season press release](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3344/) and reflected in the broader daily mini-game updates in [v6.0.0 Version Info](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/page/v6/versioninfo_v6-00).

## Why it matters for grinders

Daily modes are usually low effort / steady currency. A refreshed mini-game loop is Konami’s way of keeping the daily login habit sticky during the new season campaign window.

## PixelPitch approach

- Clear **Daily Super Play** before Division if you are tilt-prone — bank the free progress first
- Treat it as a warm-up for timing, not a substitute for Game Plan practice
- Do not skip campaign login presents while chasing mini-game high scores

## Context

This sits under the season theme **“Play On, All Together.”** — Konami’s framing for features aimed at both veterans and newer players ([official news](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/topic/news/5558)).
