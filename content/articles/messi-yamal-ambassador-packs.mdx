Konami’s new season content drop includes **Ambassador Editions and Packs** built around eFootball ambassadors **Lionel Messi** and **Lamine Yamal**. Official press notes that these packs can include players, development items, stadium customisation, and — in Deluxe Editions — special team kits ([Konami announcement](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3344/)).

## What is confirmed

- New ambassador-themed editions / packs with the season launch
- Mix of players + progression items + stadium cosmetics
- Deluxe tiers adding kits

Exact odds, individual card ratings, and shop prices live in the client and can change — always verify in-game before spending coins or money.

## PixelPitch spend framework

1. **Empty the New Season Campaign first** — free contracts and tokens reduce the need to buy progression filler.
2. **Buy for the player, not the kit** — cosmetics do not win Division.
3. **Compare to your starting XI hole** — a pack that duplicates your best AMF is entertainment, not squad building.
4. **Set a coin floor** before opening anything.
