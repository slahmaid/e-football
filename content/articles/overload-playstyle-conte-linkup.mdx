Two tactical headline pieces landed with Konami’s season update: a new **Overload** Team Playstyle, and managers who carry **two Link-Up Plays** for the first time.

## Overload, as Konami describes it

Official pre-season notes say **Overload** concentrates players on the ball side to create numerical superiority ([official news](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/topic/news/5558)):

- **In attack** — shorter passing lanes and better possession in crowded zones
- **In defence** — a compact shape that closes the ball carrier faster

That is a natural partner for Fluid Formation: overload to progress, then snap into a tighter defensive block when the ball turns over.

## Dual Link-Up managers

Konami’s press coverage calls out legendary coach **Antonio Conte** arriving with two Link-Up Skills: **Touchdown Pass C** and **One-Two Cut-In B** ([Konami announcement](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3344/)). Official news also confirmed managers with two Link-Up Plays as a first for the title ([feature preview](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/topic/news/5558)).

## How to test them fairly

1. Play ten matches with your current manager as a baseline.
2. Switch to Conte (or another dual Link-Up manager) without changing formation.
3. Track only assisted goals from Link-Up patterns — not overall scoreline noise.

If Link-Up goals do not rise, the manager is not the problem; your trigger players or instructions are.
