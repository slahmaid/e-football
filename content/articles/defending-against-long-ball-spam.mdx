Long-ball spam is annoying because it skips your midfield. The answer is not a six-at-the-back bunker — it is a mid-block that owns the second ball and punishes the clearance.

## What the spammer wants

They want:

- Your defensive line pinned deep
- A footrace onto a lofted pass
- Panic clearances that restart the loop

Deny the footrace and the tactic collapses.

## Shape that works

Use a **mid-block 4-2-3-1** or **4-3-3 holding**:

- Defensive line: slightly higher than default, not maximum.
- Compactness: high — force play into crowded zones.
- Full-backs: stay back while attacking so the first clearance does not find empty flanks.

## Individual instructions

- **Both CBs:** Defensive — no stepping up into midfield for glory interceptions.
- **DMF:** Anchor Man, cover centre.
- **AMF / SS:** Tracking Back on — they must help when the ball is launched.

## In-match habits

1. Do not rush the first defender at the passer; cut the landing zone.
2. Win the aerial, then play a simple sideways pass — not another hopeful long ball.
3. If you clear under pressure, clear **wide and long**, not straight back to their ten.

## When to park the bus

Only in the final ten minutes while protecting a one-goal lead against a known spammer. Even then, keep one outlet forward so you are not inviting endless corners.

Long balls feel unstoppable until the second ball belongs to you. Own that, and the spam becomes free possession.
