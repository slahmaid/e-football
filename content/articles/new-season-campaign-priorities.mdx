Konami opened the new season with a time-limited **New Season Campaign** running **13 August–3 September 2026**, alongside the major update. Official channels describe login bonuses, campaign objectives, and VS AI events that pay development items, GP, EXP, and more.

## What Konami is promoting

From the [Konami season announcement](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3344/):

- Campaign window celebrating the new season
- Rewards via **login bonuses** and **campaign objectives**
- A **Special Player Contract** for logging in — select from a pool featuring stars tied to FC Barcelona and Manchester United (per that announcement)
- Additional acquisition / development rewards for completing objectives

Earlier official messaging framed the season under the concept **“Play On, All Together.”** ([official news](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/topic/news/5558)).

## PixelPitch priority order

1. **Log in daily** — campaign login presents are designed around streak behaviour; missing days is free value left on the table.
2. **Finish Round objectives as they unlock** — official campaign schedules have added challenge waves during the window; clear Round 1 before Round 2 piles up.
3. **VS AI event points** — use flexible match levels if offered so you can finish challenges without tilting your Division form.
4. **Pack FOMO last** — ambassador packs and deluxe editions are marketing layers; clear free campaign currency first.

## Reminder

Dates and reward tables can shift by region or live ops patches. Always confirm the in-client event screen and Konami’s official topic pages before grinding.
