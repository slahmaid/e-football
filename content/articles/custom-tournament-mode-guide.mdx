**Custom Tournament** is one of the clearest “play together” features in Konami’s new season. Official materials describe knock-out brackets for up to **eight** players, with host-defined themes and the option to compete in Dream Team or under Friend Match–style regulations.

## What you can do

From the [official v6.0.0 Version Info](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/page/v6/versioninfo_v6-00):

- **Host** a tournament or **join** one created by someone else
- Restrict eligible players by league, club, country, region, and similar themes
- Keep playing other modes while a tournament is running (with notices for your bracket)
- As organiser, spectate participant matches in real time (also covered in [Konami’s season announcement](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3344/))

## Formats that work for groups

1. **Same-club only** — everyone builds around one licensed club identity.
2. **League lock** — e.g. only Serie A / LaLiga players for a themed night.
3. **Budget Dream Team** — house rule on total GP or no featured pack spam (community rule, not a Konami toggle).
4. **Beginner bracket** — lighter time controls and clearer kickoff times for newer players.

## Hosting checklist

- Publish the restriction list **before** invites
- Agree on no-show rules
- Keep one open slot for a late friend if your group is inconsistent
- Spectate finals if you are hosting — Konami built that for a reason
