September stacks login rewards, pack windows, and free agent trials on top of each other. Chase everything and you burn time. Chase value and you finish the month stronger.

## Priority ladder

### Tier S — do these first

- **Daily login streak** — the cumulative coin total beats most one-off packs if you stay consistent.
- **Match-pass milestones** — complete the easy objectives early; leave the grindy ones for weekends.
- **Free agent tryouts with clear positional need** — only if the player fits a hole in your starting XI.

### Tier A — worth your evening

- Limited-time coin boosters tied to match counts.
- Exchange programs where duplicates convert into useful trainers or contracts.

### Tier C — skip unless you enjoy gambling

- Premium packs marketed around a single featured name you already own in a better version.
- Events that require ten matches for a low-chance spin.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Mon–Tue | Clear short objectives; bank coins |
| Wed–Thu | Play Division while form is fresh |
| Fri–Sun | Finish weekly missions; open rewarded packs only |

## Budget rule

Decide your September coin floor on day one. If opening a pack would drop you below that floor, do not open it — even when the banner looks urgent.

Hype expires. Coins and a balanced squad do not.
