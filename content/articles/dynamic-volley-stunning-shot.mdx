Konami added **Dynamic Volley** in **v6.0.0** so first-time strikes on aerial balls are more intentional. Official Version Info is specific: enter the **Stunning Shot** command while the ball is in the air to drive a volley toward goal — including situations where a normal shot used to become a weak header.

## Official behaviour (plain English)

Per the [v6.0.0 Version Info](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/page/v6/versioninfo_v6-00):

- Dynamic Volley targets balls **in the air** or from lofted receives
- Height should not block the idea — the command is meant to attack mid-air balls
- Normal shot inputs on low lofted balls may now produce a natural volley more often, depending on height

The same season messaging appears in Konami’s [pre-update feature notes](https://www.konami.com/efootball/en/topic/news/5558).

## When to go for it

- Cutbacks that sit up just outside the six-yard box
- Second balls after a cleared corner
- Early crosses that arrive at knee-to-thigh height for a CF

Skip it when the ball is dropping behind your striker or when a defender is already through the line — a controlled trap still wins more Division points than a spectacular miss.

## Training drill

In training, feed lofted passes into the box and only finish with Stunning Shot for ten reps. Then mix normal shots. Your brain needs the command mapping before online pressure hits.
